Building an AI-Powered HR Compliance Platform

Website & App Development

Project Overview

88Pilot is an AI-powered HR compliance platform built to help US businesses navigate the complexities of workplace regulations. Kyoto Creative worked on creating a digital product that brings compliance intelligence, policy management, and essential HR workflows into one connected experience, making a traditionally complex process feel simpler and more accessible.

Building a Smarter Future for HR Compliance

Key focus areas

  • AI-powered compliance workflows
  • SaaS platform experience
  • HR technology integrations
  • Compliance monitoring and reporting
  • Workflow automation
Visit site

The Challenge

HR compliance is rarely straightforward. Constantly changing regulations, scattered documentation, employee training requirements, and state-specific rules can leave businesses struggling to stay organised and prepared. The challenge was to bring these moving parts together in a way that reduces complexity, improves visibility, and helps teams move from reactive compliance management to a more proactive approach.

The Solution

We helped shape a connected SaaS experience that brings AI-powered assistance, compliance monitoring, policy workflows, and business integrations into one platform. From simplifying access to regulatory information to organising compliance tasks and connecting existing HR tools, the focus was on creating a product that transforms complexity into clarity and gives businesses a smarter way to manage compliance.

What We Built

The platform brings together an intuitive SaaS interface, AI-assisted compliance workflows, regulatory intelligence, policy management tools, compliance dashboards, and third-party integrations. Each element is designed to work as part of a connected ecosystem, helping businesses organise critical information, identify areas requiring attention, and manage compliance with greater confidence.
