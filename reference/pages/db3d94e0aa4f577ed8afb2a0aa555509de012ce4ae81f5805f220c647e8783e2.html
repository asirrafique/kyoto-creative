Successful start of the year at KOTA

February 8th 2021

By Maja

2020, what a year it was!

From the global pandemic and the need to quickly adapt to the new circumstances, to growing our team and deciding on making remote working a more permanent change for us…

2020 has also brought us a lot of exciting partnerships and many projects we’re really proud of. Starting 2021 we’re setting the bar even higher than before and look forward to even more amazing stuff. But before we do that, let’s spend a little moment celebrating our most recent achievements.

 

KOTA named one of the Top B2B Companies for the second year in a row.

In their recent ranking, Clutch, a leading market research firm, has named KOTA one of the top B2B companies in the UK  and not for the first time either! For the second year in a row, we were featured as part of the Creative & Design section and we couldn’t be more proud.

 

SuperYachtsMonaco is getting Special Kudos

Our most recent launch – a beautiful website for SuperYachtsMonaco has received great recognition in the web design sector. With a fresh creative approach to the yacht industry, this website definitely stands out from the crowd.

We’ve received:

  • An ‘Honorable Mention’ on AWWWARDS
  • ‘Special Kudos’, UX, UI & Innovation Awards on CSS Design Awards
  • ‘Site of the Day’ from Mindsparkle Magazine
  • ‘Featured of the Day’ on CSSLight
  • ‘Site of the Day’ on Best CSS

 

Adams & Butler featured on Communication Arts.

Another last year’s favourite, the high-end travel experiences platform we created for Adams & Butler is also getting the hype! With an intuitive user experience and impeccable design, there’s no surprise in that. We’re proud to have been featured in the ‘Webpicks’ section on Communication Arts.

Martin Building Co.

Last but not least of our latest recognition is the elegant portfolio for California-based Martin Building Company.

Thanks to its sleek design and cutting-edge animation, we’ve received plenty of features:

  • An ‘Honorable Mention’ on AWWWARDS
  • ‘Special Kudos’, UX, UI & Innovation Awards on CSS Design Awards
  • ‘Webpick’ on Communication Arts
  • ‘Featured of the Day’ on CSSLight
  • ‘Site of the Day’ on Best CSS
